# Authoring Workflow Specs

Rules for text that lands in workflow specs (`*-projex.md`), `SKILL.md`, or this file — loaded every session, so every surviving sentence costs forever. Spec text keeps full grammatical sentences with zero filler, hedging, or self-defense. Documents produced *by* workflows follow `SKILL.md § Authoring` (Dehydrate, De-slop) instead.

- **Straight talk the subject** — a section opens with its own content. No framing about what other sections or components do.
- **Bare rules** — `**Lead** — clause`, one line per rule, at most one reason clause. No worked arguments, no anticipated objections, no carve-outs for what the wording already excludes.
- **No author voice** — justification, comparisons, and "why this way" belong to the plan or proposal that landed the change. The spec states; it does not defend.
- **Content lives where it is used** — templates and conventions embed in the workflow that uses them; `SKILL.md` carries framework-wide policy only. Canonical home = the section that historically owns the concern.
- **Structure over prose** — tables, templates, and vocabularies stay; compression targets sentences, not operational content.
